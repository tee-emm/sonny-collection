These are comparison subtotals, not an asking price, appraisal or expected sale proceeds. Most sources are asking prices. Dates and condition matches vary, and neither subtotal covers the entire collection.

How to read these figures

The raw subtotal uses the first priced reference for each covered entry and includes some bundles, mismatched variants and unusually high asking prices. The filtered subtotal uses directly read per-unit comparisons for the same or a similar item, excluding known wrong-condition matches, bundles and excerpt-only or unavailable sources. Condition and event dates can still be uncertain.

For example, the Clown on Stilts Robby source is an active US$3,000 asking listing, rather than a verified completed sale. High-value pieces need stronger sold-price evidence before a firm valuation can be supported.
